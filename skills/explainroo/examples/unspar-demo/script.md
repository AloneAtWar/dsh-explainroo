# Unspar

## hook
[#logo] This is Unspar. [#writes] It writes articles for your website, like [#blog] blog posts and [#guides] guides.

## brief
First, [#tell] you tell it what to write about. Paste your [#site] website, [#pick] choose what you want to make and [#topic] add a topic. [#ideas] Unspar looks at your site and drafts a few ideas.

## ideas
A moment later, [#list] you have ideas to choose from. You [#choose] pick the one you like.

## price
[#price] Before anything runs, you see what it costs. [#queue] When you {queue|cue} it, the credits are set aside and the work starts.

## agents
Then [#agents] its AI agents get to work. They [#research] look up the topic, [#write] write the article in your voice and [#check] check the facts.

## voice
[#voice] You decide what that voice sounds like. Pick how [#formal] formal it is and [#rhythm] how the sentences flow.

## publish
When you're happy with it, [#publish] Unspar puts it on your site. [#live] It works with WordPress and Ghost.

## outro {hold=1}
[#brand] Unspar. Content that doesn't read like AI. [#try] Try it for free at {unspar.com|unspar dot com}.
